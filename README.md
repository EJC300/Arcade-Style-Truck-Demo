# Arcade Style Truck Demo
Originally tried to make a game based on an old truck game. 

****
About:
I originally set off to make a mid sized open world driving game similar to a trucking game series from the early 2000s. Which involved driving a semi arcade truck and delivering cargo to make money in order to upgrade your truck and expand your business within a region of unknown name. I spent the next few weeks after deciding to embark on this project while still in college to research how wheeled vehicles would work within the confines of arcade style video games with simulation elements. I then began work on it starting with this demo. I one day still plan on finishing the original project which was set during a pandemic within a dystopian region with similar gameplay to the aforementioned trucking game. But Here is the original prototype. I have decided to redo the demo to work more intuitively and fix several things which are mentioned in this doc. 
***

Originally Planned Features:
1. One Region inspired by the American Midwest during a pandemic dystopia(Totally unrelated to Covid this was before the pandemic.)
2. Four trucks to drive and upgrade
3. Different types of cargo and trailers
4. Light business sim elements
5. Bandits that will chase you which you can bribe.
6. Truck races with cargo.
7. Open Ended
8. Arcade Style Forgiving Yet Forgiving Driving
9. Competitors 
10. Police
11. Secret Areas

___
Features of the Demo:
1. Three Demo Scenes, Driving, AI Truck Driving Demonstration, and a Main Menu
2. Demonstration of the Arcade Driving Physics
3. Shader Effects and Post Fx
4. Demonstration of AI Truck maneuvering.
5. Engine SFX
6. Main Menu Prototype to tie everything together
7. Graphics Effects
